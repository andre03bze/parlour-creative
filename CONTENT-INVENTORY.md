## Voice reference (apply to any fresh copy)

Three words: **Intelligent. Distinctive. Commercial.** (playbook line 52). Say/never-say
table at lines 53–64 — e.g. say "one marketing system," never "full-service /
360-degree / one-stop shop"; say "senior-led, accountable," never "passionate team,
bespoke solutions"; say "specific numbers, named places," never "disruptive,
game-changing, cutting-edge." Applies to every word written for `/services`,
`/approach`, `/belize`, and any metadata/SEO copy not directly quoted from the
playbook.
